# Trade & Growth Simulator

A simulation game where villages collect resources and develop through trade on a randomly generated continent. Players observe village growth as roads develop based on traffic and settlements evolve from villages to towns to cities.

## Core Concept
- **Observation-based gameplay**: Players primarily watch and observe rather than directly control
- **Resource-driven economy**: Villages collect food, wood, and ore from surrounding tiles
- **Trade networks**: Villages exchange resources via road networks that evolve based on usage
- **Population dynamics**: Villages grow or decline based on resource availability and consumption
- **Supply & demand system**: Real-time resource production, consumption, and storage management

## Key Features
- **2D tile-based map** with different terrain types (plains, forest, mountains, water, roads)
- **Resource depletion and recovery** system where tiles lose resources when harvested but recover over time
- **Automatic trade routes** between villages based on supply/demand imbalances
- **Building construction** tied to population growth and resource availability
- **God mode intervention** allowing players to directly adjust resources
- **Real-time monitoring** of village status and resource flows

## Target Experience
A relaxing "city builder" where players watch organic growth patterns emerge from economic simulation, similar to watching an ant colony or ecosystem develop naturally.
